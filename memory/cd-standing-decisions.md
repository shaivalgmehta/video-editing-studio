Standing decisions from ad 2A (Oct 2026), to apply to every Delesign ad without re-asking:
- No source footnote on the $85,000 figure; no blurring of client names, logos or people in Go screens. Both override the brief's legal rules. Log them in the QA report each time.
- Keep face time to the hook and the close; the middle section is Shaival in a small picture-in-picture with the graphics owning the frame on a light dot-pattern ground.
- No hand-drawn circles on screenshots. Clean selection outlines, or play the screen recording full-bleed.
- A representative number ("× 6 clients") stays off screen.
- A word that isn't said clearly gets its sentence cut; never patch with AI voice or re-synthesis. Exception set on 2D (Oct 2026): when the flub is in the hook line ("phone number I ever done"), the CD preferred the full line as spoken over a trimmed sentence ("the cut one looks bad"). Offer both, default to full.
- Go screens: when the CD supplies a capture with a built-in magnified callout (e.g. `Feedback after.jpg`), frame the window on the callout and put a clean orange outline on it; label bottom-left when bottom-right is under the picture-in-picture.
- Open on real motion (Shaival walking in and sitting down) rather than on the first word; the hook bar is on from frame 1.
- Real Go screenshots beat mock panels, even at 1x.

**Why:** each came as a direct note on a review render; they're taste and policy calls the CD owns.
**How to apply:** build them in from the start; mention them once in the QA report as brief deviations. Details in [[delesign-ad-pipeline]].
