- B-roll: Pexels and Pixabay video pages carry direct file URLs in their "Edit in Canva"/"Edit video" links; both licenses allow ads without credit. Golden Gate set and sources in `assets/broll/golden_gate/LICENSES.md`.
- Music: Mixkit tag pages (`https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/tag/<tag>/`) list direct `assets.mixkit.co/music/<id>/<id>.mp3` links; Mixkit license covers commercial use, no credit. House bed: 749; alternate: 139.
- Logos: take the client's own SVG/PNG from their site or brand kit; real third-party logos from Wikimedia Commons (see the stock-sourcing skill).
- Fonts: Google Fonts GitHub (`google/fonts` repo, `ofl/hankengrotesk`, `ofl/architectsdaughter`), OFL licensed.
- SFX: HyperFrames media-use bundle at `.agents/skills/media-use/audio/assets/sfx/` (click-soft is the house click).
