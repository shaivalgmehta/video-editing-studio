If the storyboard or brief asks for something (e.g. "brand names flash past under step 2"), include it. Don't leave it out on my own caution (trademark worries etc.) and just flag it.

**Why:** on a motion-graphics job I once skipped the Android brand names and the CD corrected me: "if it's asked for, include it".
**How to apply:** build every requested element; use only the names/facts the storyboard itself gives (no invented extras). Concerns go to the CD as a note, not an omission.
