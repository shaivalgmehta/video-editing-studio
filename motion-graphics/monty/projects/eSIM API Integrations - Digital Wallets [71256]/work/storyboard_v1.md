## Shots (timings from the storyboard; re-timed to the VO once we have it)

1. **Hook, 0:00–0:04**: "Your users already plan their trips in your wallet."
   - Dot grid fades up, logo top-left, eyebrow "eSIM API · FOR DIGITAL WALLETS" letters in.
   - "Your users travel / with your wallet." rises word by word; marker bar wipes under line 2.
   - Phone swings in from the right, tilted; balance $1,240.00 counts up; "Upcoming trip · Paris" row slides in.
   - Three ticket cards deal in, one after another (FX exchange €400, Flight booked $612, Hotel paid $520); PARIS outline strokes draw in behind them.
   - Out: cards flick off; phone slides right; PARIS outline slides away as ROAMING slides in.
2. **The gap, 0:04–0:08**: "Then they land, and buy data somewhere else."
   - "Then they land / and pay someone else." with a coral marker bar.
   - Three red-icon tickets drop in (Airport SIM, Roaming, Another app).
   - "PAID ELSEWHERE · REVENUE LOST" stamp slams on "somewhere else" with a small camera shake.
   - Out: a radar ring pulses from the right edge and sweeps the frame clean.
3. **Trip detected, 0:08–0:13**: "...your wallet spots the trip and offers data at the right moment."
   - Location pin pops; radar rings pulse out; "TRIP DETECTED · NYC TO PARIS" stamp lands on "spots the trip".
   - "Spot the trip. / Offer the data." with a marker bar.
   - Push notification slides in ("Your Wallet · now · Heading to Paris?…"); France 10 GB · $13 row lights up on "offers data".
   - Out (match cut): the notification grows into the phone's "Confirm purchase" card.
4. **Pay from balance, 0:13–0:18**: "Users pay from their balance in one tap. No card, no second checkout."
   - Phone on its backing panel; "Pay from balance. / One tap."; 1 TAP outline.
   - Tap ripple on "one tap"; the button squashes; the balance ticks down; a receipt card slides out showing the strikethrough old balance → new.
   - Chips "No card needed" and "No second checkout" pop in on "No card" and "no second checkout".
   - Out: the same phone stays; the screen dims to the lock screen.
5. **Online on landing, 0:18–0:22**: "They land online. And when data runs low, they top up in the app."
   - Lock screen 09:15 · Paris CDG; 5G pill blinks on; "You're online in France." notification; "ARRIVED · PARIS CDG · ONLINE" stamp.
   - Data bar drains to 20% on "runs low"; "Top up 5 GB · $7" button presses; the bar refills.
   - Out: the phone drops off the bottom of the frame; the cards rise into scene 6.
6. **What the wallet gets, 0:22–0:27**: "You earn on every bundle, keep balances in your wallet, and give users one more reason to open your app."
   - "New revenue. / More reasons to open your app."
   - EARN / KEEP / GROW cards land one per clause; the line chart draws, the coins flip, the bars grow (no numbers). +REVENUE outline behind.
   - Out: the cards fold down; the line shoots across into scene 7.
7. **Easy to integrate, 0:27–0:31**: "One API covers it all, in 200-plus countries and regions."
   - "One API. / 200+ countries and regions."
   - Gradient line draws left→right; each node (Catalog, Order, Activate, Top up, Webhooks) pops with its label as the line reaches it. API outline.
   - Out: the line pulls in to the centre and the frame clears.
8. **End card, 0:31–0:35**: "Monty eSIM. Travel data, built into your wallet."
   - "Travel data, / built into your wallet." with the marker bar sweep; montyesim.com types on; API stamp rolls in; WALLET outline.
   - Hold about 1 s on the full frame.

Sound: VO, music bed with its drop on scene 3 (the turn from problem to solution) and the final hit on the end card; whoosh on each transition; thud on stamps; click on taps; tick on number changes.
