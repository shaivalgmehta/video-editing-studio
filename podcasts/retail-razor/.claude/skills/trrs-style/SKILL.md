# TRRS style kit

This is the visual and sound system the CD approved on S6E15 (Oct–Nov 2026). Generic caption, mix and QA craft lives in
the L1/L2 skills (`video-edit-core`, `video-qa-delivery`, `podcast-reel`). This file only fixes the TRRS values.
The standing CD decisions are in `podcasts/retail-razor/CLAUDE.md`. Read them as well.

| Ground (16:9 end card / reel outro) | `radial-gradient(ellipse 70% 80% at 32% 45%, #4a2a1d 0%, #2a1610 45%, #120a07 100%)` |
| Page / letterbox | `#120a07` |

Don't use the media-kit system (#16171B, Archivo Black, Space Mono) for video. The CD reverted it on teaser v2.

## Type
- **Montserrat** (variable, use 800–900): headlines, captions, card text, labels. Labels are 800, 28–32 px,
  letter-spacing .14–.18em, uppercase, peach.
- **Caveat 700**: reel name tags.
- **Caveat Brush**: teaser handwriting (names with drawn arrows, "X → Y" write-ons). The CD rejected Mr Dafoe.
- 28 px is the minimum size for any copy that has to be read. Smaller text is only acceptable as UI texture inside a
  rebuilt app screen.
- Fonts are in every template's `assets/fonts/` (Google Fonts, OFL).

## Logo
- `assets/img/rr_logo.png` is the full lock-up with the tagline. `rr_mark.png` is the R alone.
- **Reels:** top-left at left 60 / top 132, height 112, `drop-shadow(0 2px 10px rgba(0,0,0,.6))`
  (`compositions/header.html`). Don't add any "The Retail Razor Show" text or headline next to it. It is **hidden over
  every full-screen b-roll clip and full-screen card**. Keep the `BROLL` list in `header.html` in sync with the
  timeline, cut for cut.
- **Teaser:** on the end card (height 150, top-left) and inside the client's cover on the title card.
- **Covers:** height 96 at left 60 / top 318, with "THE RETAIL RAZOR SHOW / SxEyy" in peach beside it.

## Reel layout (1080x1920, 30 fps)
- Speaker window `#win`: left 0, top 325, 1080 x 1270, overflow hidden, with
  `box-shadow: 0 -2px 0 rgba(227,38,43,.6), 0 2px 0 rgba(227,38,43,.6), 0 0 120px rgba(227,38,43,.16)`.
- Captions band: top 1290 (below the window's lower third). Card content stays above y 1250.
- Name tag: left 60 / top 1146 (lower-left of the window, above the captions).
- B-roll and cards are full screen at 1080x1920 (`object-fit: cover`), z above the window. The logo is hidden over them.

## Captions
- **Reels:** generated by `templates/reel/tools/build_captions.py`. Uppercase Montserrat 900, 76 px, letter-spacing
  -.02em, cream with a black shadow stack. Max 3 words, 16 characters and 1.2 s per line, and lines break on
  punctuation. The spoken word lights peach and then settles to cream. 6–12 **key words per reel stay red** (`--key`).
  Each line pops in with a 0.14 s scale .92→1 `back.out(2)`. They stay on over b-roll and cards.
- **Teaser:** generated by `templates/teaser/tools/build_captions.py`. Sentence case, Montserrat 800, 54 px, at top 930,
  max 5 words and 1.8 s per line. Lines never span a cut. The cut-off final line ends on "…".
- Always verbatim to the final audio: re-transcribe the delivered voice.wav and diff it against words.json. Show-notes
  transcripts drift (S6E15 notes ran about 4 s early and dropped words).

## Name tags (reels)
Caveat 700 84 px cream name with Montserrat 800 28 px peach uppercase role, `letter-spacing .16em`. They slide in from
-24 px (0.35 s expo.out, role 0.12 s later), stay on about 3.4 s, and fade in 0.2 s. Each speaker gets a tag the first
time they're on camera. Hosts: "Ricardo Belmar / Host, The Retail Razor Show", "Casey Golden / Co-host, The Retail Razor
Show". Guest: name / role exactly as the episode credits them.

## Explainer cards
The pattern is `templates/reel/compositions/card.html` (R2's rank card): dark scrim, peach tracked label, cream chip
that turns red on the key word, numbered rows that move ON the spoken words. Other approved S6E15 card types:
- legacy-brand logo wall with sourced founding years (R2)
- rebuilt Reddit thread with upvote stamp (R1)
- AI answer ranking / mirror / cracked mirror / scattered team cards aligning (R3)
- product grid, rules checklist, silos, human-vs-crawler split (R4)
- partner badges with real platform logos, funnel with no numbers, promo lock-screen (R5)
- old vs new purchase path, AI search box, Reddit + AI checks (R6)

Motion should explain the argument, not decorate it, and every beat lands on a word time from words.json.

## Reel outro (`templates/reel/compositions/outro.html`)
3.00 s, host id `outro`, z-index 20, mounted at the end of the reel and never restyled. A razor-blade slash wipes
diagonally from lower-left to upper-right and reveals the ground. Then the logo lands, followed by "Cutting through the
clutter", a red rule, "FULL EPISODE", the episode title in Montserrat 900 70 px with a red period, the show/episode
line, the four platform icons and a red "Follow for more →" pill. Per episode you change only the variables
`ep_title` (the full-episode title) and `ep_show` ("The Retail Razor Show · SxEyy"). There is no exit animation
because reels loop. Its `line_unread` check warnings are expected.

## Sound
- Voice: cleaned (highpass 80 Hz, gentle de-ess, light compression, limiter) to **-16 LUFS**, about -2.3 dBTP, 48 kHz
  mono. Cuts happen at word boundaries with 10–20 ms crossfades.
- Reel music: Mixkit "Close Up" (1167) at **volume 0.2** under the voice, plus a second clip of the same file at
  **0.55** for the outro, continuing the same media time. Choose the bed's `data-media-start` so the outro starts on a
  bar line (groove at media 18.3 s, bar 2.286 s). This bed is a stand-in until the show-theme licence (imunobeats)
  arrives.
- SFX, used sparingly:
  - `whoosh.wav` 0.2–0.35 on b-roll/card cuts (`whoosh2` for a softer one)
  - `click.wav` 0.16–0.3 on card beats
  - `impact.wav` about 0.2 on a single payoff
  - `glitch.wav` 0.25–0.3 on teaser seams and on cracks
  - `type.wav` 0.28 under typing
  - `shing.wav` 0.8 at **outro start − 0.33 s** (its transient lands with the blade)
- Delivery: master each render to -14 LUFS integrated, ≤ -1.5 dBTP (workspace rule).
