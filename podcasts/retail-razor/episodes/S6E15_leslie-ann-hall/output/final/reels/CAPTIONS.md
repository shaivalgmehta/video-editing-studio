## R5 — Bottom funnel bloat · post Nov 10
File: TRRS_S6E15_R5_9x16_v1.mp4

**YouTube Shorts title:** Is your ad budget suffering from "bottom funnel bloat"?

**Instagram caption / YouTube description:**
Iced Media is a Meta, TikTok and Google partner, and Leslie Ann Hall is still warning brands about this.

She calls it "bottom funnel bloat": an outsized share of spend going to conversion media, paying more to get existing customers to buy more often, and leaning on promotions and sales, all at the expense of finding new customers.

Ricardo Belmar and Leslie get into why brand marketing matters more than ever.

## R4 — 200 products, 3 rules · post Nov 13

**YouTube Shorts title:** 3 rules for winning in AI search

**Instagram caption / YouTube description:**
Got 200 products? You "might not deserve to rank for everything."

Leslie Ann Hall's three rules for winning in AI search:
1. Be intentional about a handful of hero products you really have the right to rank for.
2. Get otherwise siloed teams working together with the same message.
3. Make sure your content is understood by both humans and machines.
