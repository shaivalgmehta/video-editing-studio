## What the reference does (style to borrow)
- It opens cold on the guest's boldest claim, with huge kinetic type that slams in, blurs in and glows.
- Guest credentials arrive about 15 s in, on a bold title card with a cutout portrait.
- Host question then guest answer, building to a "wait, hold on" reaction from the host.
- Motion graphics show the ideas themselves: a search bar typing, data cards, chalk-style handwriting, a funnel diagram.
- Glitch and channel-split transitions, punch-ins, B&W freeze frames and a driving music bed.
- It ends on a line cut off mid-sentence, so the answer stays hidden.

## Brand mapping
- The reference's yellow glow becomes the Retail Razor red, plus the warm peach glow from the cover.
- The base is black and deep brown, taken from the cover.
- Type is a heavy sans in white, with a script accent like the cover's "Is".
- The title card is the cover headline, "Is AI Ignoring Your Brand?", with the Leslie cutout from the cover.
- The end card uses the R logo, "Stay sharp. Stay human. Stay ahead.", the release date and @retailrazor.
