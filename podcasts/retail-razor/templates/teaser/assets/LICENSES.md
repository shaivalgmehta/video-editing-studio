# Asset sources and licences: TRRS teaser template

Copy into the episode's teaser project and ADD every episode asset (music source + stems, cover, cutout, logos, b-roll),
each checked on its own source page.

## Music
Not included: each teaser's bed is edited to its own beat map (see the trrs-teaser skill). The approved S6E15 v5 used
Mixkit 370 "Sparta" by Arulo (Mixkit Stock Music Free License), edited to 60 s, with hits in a separate hits.wav.
The v6/v7 beds (Mixkit 548, Mixkit 550 stems) were unapproved explorations. Log the track you use.
